6. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Technical Architecture & Zero-Cost Strategy

⚙️ Backend & Database
  • PHP 8.x + Native PDO: High execution speed, low memory footprint, zero framework overhead.
  • MySQL / MariaDB: Robust relational schema, prepared statements, and UTF8mb4 bilingual encoding.
  • XAMPP Ready: Tested locally in Windows XAMPP root, 100% portable to cPanel/Linux production.
📺 Zero-Cost YouTube Sync
  • No Google Cloud Billing: Completely bypasses quota limits, credit cards, and complex OAuth setups.
  • URL & ID Auto-Parser: Editors simply paste links; system extracts the 11-character video ID.
  • HQ Thumbnails Auto-Fetched: Dynamically loads high-res previews directly from YouTube CDN.
📈 Market Data & Caching
  • Hybrid Free API + 15m Cache: Lightweight market quotes cached locally to prevent rate limiting.
  • CMS Manual Overrides: Editorial admins can manually adjust Sensex & Nifty during holidays or downtime.
  • Zero Downtime Fallback: Public pages remain blazing fast even if external feeds disconnect.

9. QUALITY & PERFORMANCE

Non-Functional Specifications: Speed, SEO & Security

⚡ Speed & WCAG AA UX
  • Sub-2.0s LCP & CLS < 0.05: Lightweight HTML5/Tailwind with fixed aspect ratios and zero framework bloat.
  • WCAG AA Accessibility (US-25): Visible keyboard focus rings, ≥44×44px touch targets, semantic HTML, and text form errors.
  • Motion & Speed: Honors prefers-reduced-motion while keeping PHP PDO TTFB under 30ms.
🔍 SEO & Social Virality
  • Dynamic OpenGraph Tags: Pre-rendered preview cards for viral sharing across WhatsApp and X.
  • NewsArticle Schema: Schema.org structured markup for Google News indexing.
  • LocalBusiness Studio Schema: Dominates local Google search for "video studio on rent Palwal Delhi NCR" (sndmedia.in).
🛡️ Security & Anti-Abuse
  • SQLi & XSS Defense: 100% PDO prepared statements, htmlspecialchars() plain-text escaping, and allowlist HTML sanitizer.
  • Anti-Bot Protection: CSRF tokens, invisible honeypot traps, and 5/hr IP rate limiting on rental forms.
  • Auth Hardening: BCRYPT (≥12), 1-hr cryptographic password reset tokens, HttpOnly/HTTPS cookies & RBAC guards.
